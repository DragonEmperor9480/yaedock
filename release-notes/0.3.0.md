YaeDock 0.3.0 adds three new features: the phone can show the PC's screen, and the PC can use the phone's microphone and camera.

## New

### The PC's screen on the phone
Open **Controls** and tap the laptop icon. There are two ways to use it:
- **Mirror a screen:** the phone shows one of your monitors.
- **Extended screen:** the phone becomes one more display beside your monitors. You can drag windows onto it.

The first time, the PC asks which screen to share; after that it starts without asking. The gear button switches between the two ways and sets the size: full size, 1080p, 720p or 540p.

### The phone as the PC's microphone
Tap the microphone icon in **Controls**. The PC gets a new microphone named after the phone, such as "Pixel 6 microphone", which you can choose in any program's sound settings. It keeps working while the app is in the background. The sound is compressed, so it needs little Wi-Fi.

### The phone as the PC's camera (beta)
Tap the camera icon in **Controls** and choose the back or front camera. The PC gets a camera named after the phone, which browsers, OBS Studio and Kamoso can use. The same menu sets the size: 720p, 480p, 360p or 240p.
- It starts at 240p. Sizes above 360p are the least steady.
- On a weak signal it lowers its quality by itself, and skips pictures rather than showing old ones.
- It's in beta: it may not always work as expected, and with the camera and microphone both on, the microphone may glitch.

### `yaedock check`
- **New lines:** Screen, Phone mic and Phone camera. Each says what works on your PC and names what to install.
- **Camera test:** the Phone camera line makes a test camera for a moment and reads a picture back, so it catches real problems, not only missing packages.
- **Fixing problems:** in the `yaedock` menu, a key runs the suggested fix commands and another copies them.

## What the PC needs

- **Screen and camera:** PipeWire and GStreamer: its tools, its PipeWire plugin, gst-plugins-bad, and an H.264 encoder or decoder. On Intel and AMD graphics that's the VA-API plugin; otherwise use gst-libav, x264 or NVIDIA's. The screen also needs a Wayland desktop with xdg-desktop-portal.
- **Microphone:** PipeWire (with pipewire-pulse) or PulseAudio. With `libopus`, which most desktops have, the sound is compressed.
- **Camera programs:** GStreamer's base plugins (`gst-plugins-base`). Without them, programs such as Kamoso show only black.

Run `yaedock check` to see what your PC has. Each icon only shows in the app when the PC can do it. The docs have the install commands for Arch, Debian, Ubuntu and Fedora: [the PC's screen](https://yaedock.amrutlabs.in/docs/#screen) and [the phone as a microphone and camera](https://yaedock.amrutlabs.in/docs/#phone-parts).

## Good to know

- **Screen:**
  - The phone only shows the picture; touching it doesn't click on the PC.
  - An extended screen needs a desktop whose portal can make a virtual display: KDE Plasma can, and GNOME should. Sway, Hyprland and X11 can't, and the app hides that option there.
- **Camera:**
  - Programs that only look for `/dev/video` devices don't find it.
  - In Firefox, turn on `media.webrtc.camera.allow-pipewire` in `about:config` if it's not listed. In Chromium browsers, turn on the "PipeWire Camera support" flag.
  - The picture is turned upright for how the phone is held when you lend the camera. After turning the phone, lend the camera again.
  - A phone that gets hot over a long session can slow its Wi-Fi a lot; a smaller size helps.
- **Microphone:** there's no echo cancelling, so use headphones on calls.
- **Everything:** only one phone at a time can be the PC's microphone, and one its camera. Video and sound travel over Wi-Fi only.

## Updating

- **PC:** run `yaedock update`, or use **Check for updates** in the `yaedock` menu, or update it from the app.
- **App:** go to About → Updates.

Both need the new version: a 0.2.0 app or PC keeps working, but without these features.
