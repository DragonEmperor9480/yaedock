YaeDock 0.6.0 keeps a history of the games you play: when you played, for how long, and how smoothly they ran. The phone shows it by day, week and month.

## New

### Play history
The PC now records every game you run with MangoHud, whether the phone is connected or not. On the phone, open it from **Played today** on Stats (in landscape, it's a button at the top).
- **Day:** the time played, a 24-hour strip showing when, and each session with its average frame rate. The game you're playing now shows as **Playing**, its time growing as you play.
- **Week:** a bar for each day, split by game, and each game's total time, sessions and average frame rate.
- **Month:** a calendar, each day darker the longer you played. Tap a day to open it.
- **‹ ›** steps back through earlier days, weeks and months.

### Each session's frame rate
Tap a session to see its frame rate minute by minute, with the 1% low and the session's average, and the CPU and GPU temperatures under it. Touch and drag along either graph to read a minute: both graphs follow your finger, so you can see what the temperatures were when the frame rate dipped.

### Rename a game
MangoHud names games after their program, such as `SB-Win64-Shipping`. Hold a session, or tap the pencil on its page, to give the game a proper name. The PC keeps it, so every phone shows the new name, for past sessions too. **Use the program name** puts the old one back.

### `yaedock play`
On the PC, `yaedock play` shows today's and this week's time, by game. `yaedock play clear` deletes the history.

### What's new, after each update
After an update, the app shows what changed once, on a page of its own. You can open it again from About.

## Better

- **`yaedock check` and `yaedock fps`** say that game frame rates also drive play history: turning one on or off does both.
- **Stats in landscape** fits on phones with a short screen: the big number sizes itself, so the graph and its times always show.

## Fixed

- **Pairing by cable without a network:** `yaedock pair` stopped with "could not find this PC's LAN address" when the PC had no network. It now pairs by USB cable instead, and says so.
- **Old MangoHud logs:** the PC sometimes kept MangoHud's logs long after a game ended. They're now deleted as soon as they're in the history.

## What the PC needs

Nothing new. Play history uses MangoHud, like game frame rates: run `yaedock fps on` once, and start games with MangoHud, for example with the Steam launch option `mangohud %command%`.

## Good to know

- **Only games run with MangoHud** are recorded.
- **Time counts while a game draws frames.** A game left paused but still drawing counts as played; a minimised one usually doesn't.
- **History starts with 0.6.0.** Games whose MangoHud logs are still on the PC when you update are picked up too.
- **It stays on your PC,** in `~/.local/share/yaedock/play/`, a few kilobytes per hour played. The phone asks for it when you open the page and keeps no copy.
- **A game appears about a minute after it quits.** Until then it shows as **Playing**.

## Updating

- **PC:** run `yaedock update`, or use **Check for updates** in the `yaedock` menu, or update it from the app.
- **App:** go to About → Updates.

Update both: play history needs 0.6.0 on the PC and in the app. An older PC is told to update when you open the page.
