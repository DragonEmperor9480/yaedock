YaeDock 0.5.0 installs with one command, runs on ARM PCs and older distributions, makes the phone speaker smoother, and lets you choose what Home shows.

## New

### Install with one command
On the PC, as yourself (not with sudo):

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/DragonEmperor9480/yaedock/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
```

It downloads YaeDock for your PC, checks it against the release's checksums, and opens the setup guide. Add `-s -- --yes` after `sh` to also start YaeDock in the background without being asked.

### ARM PCs and older distributions
- **64-bit ARM:** releases now have a program for ARM PCs, such as a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 or an ARM laptop. The install script picks it by itself, and `yaedock update` keeps it up to date.
- **Older distributions:** the PC program now runs on any distribution from about 2019 on, including Ubuntu 20.04 and 22.04 and Debian 10 to 12. Before, it needed one from 2024.

### A tile for each drive
Home shows how full each of the PC's drives is, with what's free. The bar turns amber at 90% full and red at 97%. USB drives appear within a few seconds of being plugged in. In landscape, swipe the row of tiles sideways to see them all.

### Choose what Home shows
Settings → Tabs → Home turns cards on or off: the live stats, the clock, what's playing, the quick actions, and sound and brightness. It can also turn off single tiles: CPU, RAM, network, battery and drives. What you hide gives its room to the rest. Other tabs will follow.

### Connection details
Tap the PC's name at the top of Home to see how the phone is connected:
- **On Wi-Fi:** the band (2.4, 5 or 6 GHz), the Wi-Fi generation, the link speed and the signal.
- **On the cable:** its speed, such as USB 2.0 or USB 3.
- **Both:** the delay to the PC and back.

### Controller layout of your own (beta)
In the controller, tap **Edit** to drag any stick or button where you want it and make it bigger or smaller. It's saved as the **Custom** layout in Settings, under Game controller.

### `yaedock check`
- **Install commands:** a missing package comes with the command that installs it, for pacman, apt, dnf or zypper.
- **Fixing one problem at a time:** in the `yaedock` menu, pick a problem with ↑↓ and press `a` to fix it or `c` to copy its command. `A` and `C` do all of them.
- **Long lists scroll:** the system check scrolls, and its keys stay in view.
- **A stuck GStreamer:** a new GStreamer line says when GStreamer hangs, usually after a graphics driver update, and names the plugin it's stuck on.

## Better

- **Phone speaker (beta):**
  - It plays about a ninth of a second behind the PC, down from a sixth.
  - Sound that arrives unevenly over Wi-Fi is smoothed out, so it crackles far less.
  - The PC sends sound ahead of everything else, the screen's video included.
  - It now always becomes the PC's default output. Before, the PC sometimes kept playing through the old output.
- **When the app can't connect:** after 10 seconds it suggests running `yaedock check` on the PC.
- **The bottom tab bar** no longer covers the last card on a screen.

## What the PC needs

Nothing new. The README and the [docs](https://yaedock.amrutlabs.in/docs/) now list every package YaeDock can use, with the command for each package manager, and `yaedock check` says what your PC lacks.

## Good to know

- **Phone speaker:** a video on the PC still looks slightly out of step with the sound.
- **Wi-Fi band:** if the PC menu shows the phone on 2.4 GHz and your router has 5 GHz too, moving the phone to 5 GHz makes the speaker and the screen smoother.

## Updating

- **PC:** run `yaedock update`, or use **Check for updates** in the `yaedock` menu, or update it from the app.
- **App:** go to About → Updates.

Both need the new version: a 0.4.0 app or PC keeps working, but without these features.
