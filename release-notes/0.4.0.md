YaeDock 0.4.0 connects the phone over a USB cable, brings a redesigned app, and lets the phone be the PC's keyboard and mouse, game controller and speaker.

## New

### Connecting by USB cable
Plug the phone into the PC and it moves to the cable by itself: less delay and no dropouts, with no tethering and no developer settings. Everything works over it, including the PC's screen and the phone's microphone and camera. Unplug it and the app goes back to Wi-Fi.
- **The first time:** Android asks *Open YaeDock to handle* your PC. Tap OK, and tick *Always* to skip the question next time.
- **Pairing by cable:** run `yaedock pair` and plug in a new phone while it waits. It pairs with no QR code and no Wi-Fi, and works over Wi-Fi afterwards too.
- **Copying files:** while on the cable, the phone's file transfer is off. Unplug it, or turn off *Use the USB cable* in Settings, under Controls.

### The redesign
- **Phone as:** a new tab gathers what the phone can lend the PC: its screen, microphone, speaker and camera, and its use as a keyboard, mouse or game controller. A card turns red and says On while the PC has it, and the tab shows a dot.
- **Tabs:** a bar along the bottom in portrait, and the top row of each screen in landscape. Hidden tabs come back while a finger is on the screen, or with a swipe.
- **Home:** a new layout, with the time, a greeting, the numbers you check most, what's playing, quick tiles, sound and brightness.
- **Also:** the battery is coloured by its charge on every screen, and Overview is now called Stats.

### The phone as the PC's keyboard and mouse (beta)
Open **Phone as**, then **Keyboard & mouse**, to work a PC across the room, such as one plugged into a TV.
- One finger moves the pointer; a tap clicks, and two taps double-click.
- Two fingers scroll; a tap with two fingers right-clicks. Tap, then hold and move, to drag.
- The keyboard button opens the phone's keyboard, and what you type goes to the PC.
- A row of keys has the ones phones lack: Esc, Tab, the arrows, Home, End, Page Up and Down, Delete, Insert and F1 to F12. Ctrl, Alt, Shift and Super stay held for the next key, so Ctrl then C copies.

It works on Wayland and X11, on any desktop, and asks nothing at the PC.

### The phone as a game controller (beta)
**Phone as**, then **Controller**, turns the phone sideways into a game pad that games see as a real Xbox-style controller: two sticks, a D-pad, A, B, X and Y, bumpers and triggers, and View, Menu and Guide.
- When a game rumbles, the phone vibrates; the Vibration button turns that off.
- Settings, under Game controller, sets the layout (Xbox or Symmetric) and the size of the sticks and buttons.

### The phone as the PC's speaker (beta)
Tap **Start** on the Speaker card. The PC gets an output named after the phone, such as "Pixel 6 speaker", and plays through it. Tap **Stop** and the PC goes back to the output it used before.

### `yaedock check`
New lines for the **USB cable**, **Keyboard & mouse** and **Phone speaker**. Each says whether your PC is ready, and gives the one command that sets it up when it isn't.

## What the PC needs

- **USB cable:** YaeDock's udev rule. `yaedock check` gives the command; PCs with Android's udev rules (such as the `android-udev` package) already allow it.
- **Keyboard, mouse and controller:** access to `/dev/uinput`. `yaedock check` gives the command; PCs with Steam installed usually allow it already.
- **Speaker:** PipeWire (with pipewire-pulse) or PulseAudio, the same as for the microphone.

The docs have the details: [the USB cable](https://yaedock.amrutlabs.in/docs/#cable), [the phone as a speaker](https://yaedock.amrutlabs.in/docs/#phone-parts) and [the phone as a keyboard, mouse and controller](https://yaedock.amrutlabs.in/docs/#input).

## Good to know

- **Keyboard:** typing assumes the PC uses a US keyboard layout. Letters it lacks, and emoji, are skipped.
- **Keyboard, mouse and controller:** they go away when the phone disconnects, and anything held down is let go first, so nothing stays pressed.
- **Speaker:**
  - It plays about a sixth of a second behind the PC, so a video on the PC looks slightly out of step.
  - The PC switches its default output to the phone. A program you set to a particular output stays there.
- **Everything:** one phone at a time can be the PC's speaker, and each phone is one controller.

## Updating

- **PC:** run `yaedock update`, or use **Check for updates** in the `yaedock` menu, or update it from the app.
- **App:** go to About → Updates.

Both need the new version: a 0.3.0 app or PC keeps working, but without these features.
